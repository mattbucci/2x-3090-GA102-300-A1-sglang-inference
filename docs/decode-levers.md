# Decode levers — the single-user 256K optimization ledger

The standing mission is single-user decode at 256K (tok/s, TPOT). This file is the ledger: what won, what was measured null, what is blocked and why, plus the serving-side notes (spec decoding, KV pools, thinking defaults) that the README only summarizes. Every entry carries its receipt path under `benchmarks/`.

## Fleet decode picture (server-verified at depth)

**2026-10-25 fleet refresh (300 W cap, CPU boost off, gpu-unlock on; the README's Model Support table is the receipt, `benchmarks/<preset>/results.json` the sweeps).** True-256K decoders @255K actual: **qwen36-ream 157** and **qwen35-moe 156** lead (224 / 222 short), **qwen36 129** (223 short), **nemotron3-omni 94** (101 short — the Mamba2-hybrid's near-flat depth curve is genuine: 23 recurrent layers are O(1), only 6 attention layers scale), **flint 104 on `fp8_e5m2` KV since 2026-10-31** (160 short; 93 / 166 on bf16 KV — 14 full-attention NoPE layers set the slope), the 30B-A3B MoE trio **coder-30b / coder-reap-25b / qwen3-ream ≈ 74** (207–210 short; M=1 twins — same arch class, same active experts), the Gemma hybrids **gemma4 61 / gemma4-21b-reap 62 / gemma4-12b 62 / gemma4-31b 32** (84 / 85 / 108 / 58 short — the triton split-KV cap entry below is what lifted them from 13–24), the dense DeltaNet 27Bs **qwen36-dense 52** and **qwen38 52** (73 short; M=1 twins, as the shared 48-GDN/16-attn shape predicts; qwen38 under DSpark 58 @250K), **devstral 41** (92 short; patch 062 recovered its pool to 339K). KV-capped: qwen3-vl-32b **38 @127K** (model-card cap). REAM/REAP pruning does NOT speed M=1 decode (gemma4-21b-reap ≡ gemma4, coder-reap ≡ coder-30b): activated-expert count and attention dominate, not total expert weights — the pruned variants win on KV headroom and load footprint instead.

## KV pools and context limits

‡‡ **"Max context" is the REAL KV-pool capacity** (`max_total_num_tokens` from the serve log), not the declared `--context-length`. The heavy-**VL** ships are KV-bound (dense weights + FP16 vision tower), but two config levers recover most of it: **SWA-ratio right-sizing** (the default `--swa-full-tokens-ratio 0.8` gives the sliding sub-pool 80% of full although sliding layers attend only `window=1024` — 12B 102K→565K, 26B 118K→652K) and **e5m2 FP8 KV on the triton-forced path** (31B 24K→347K combined with ratio 0.05 + MEM 0.92). devstral 202K (MEM 0.90) is full-attention-bound — no SWA pool to right-size; 262K needs MEM≈1.0. **Why the *smaller* devstral-24B caps lower than the dense gemma-4-31B (347K):** "dense" describes the FFN (no MoE routing), not the attention — and KV, the 256K bottleneck, is set by the attention layout. gemma-31B is hybrid-SWA (50/60 layers sliding at `window=1024`, only **10 full-attention**) with **MQA-4** global KV heads (`num_global_key_value_heads=4`) and `attention_k_eq_v=True` (caches K=V *once*) → **~12 KB/token**; devstral runs **full attention on all 40 layers** with GQA-8 and separate K/V → **~40 KB/token** (~3–4× heavier). At 262K that's ≈3 GB vs ≈10.5 GB of KV/GPU, so devstral exhausts the 48 GB ~3× sooner — its 0.7 GB-lighter weights don't compensate. The A3B-MoE models are genuinely 256K+ by arch: qwen36 996K / qwen36-ream 2.4M / qwen36-dense 657K / qwen3-ream 578K / Coder-A3B ~900K (fleet serve logs). Tool-use verified 1.0 to 258K true on 12B/26B/31B (probe table in the README).

## GA102 tensor-rate unlock and the power cap (2026-10-22 / 24)

**GA102 tensor-rate unlock ([Zagitta/gpu-unlock](https://github.com/Zagitta/gpu-unlock)) — LIVE since the 2026-10-22 cold boot; power cap raised 260 → 300 W on the 2026-10-24 sweep.** The module runs FWSEC-FRTS + Booter (CMP V67 signature) from the initramfs before `nvidia` binds and opens the GA102 tensor speed selectors; nothing is flashed, a power-off without `gpu_unlock.targets=all` on the kernel command line restores stock (install: dkms module + pinned 610.43.03 firmware in every initrd as `rd.driver.pre=gpu_unlock`, a `systemd-modules-load` ordering drop-in, kernel tainted by the unsigned module). **Power-cap sweep, 200 → 400 W in 10 W steps, both cards, unlock on** (`qwen38` served bare-metal at 262K, fresh prefill via `/flush_cache` before every point, 100 output tokens at 1K / 32K / 128K; `tensor_rate.py` at every cap with the server down; 2 s `nvidia-smi` + DDR5 SPD sampling; chart below, [`summary.json`](../benchmarks/power-sweep-2026-10-24/summary.json), raw [`serve-results.json`](../benchmarks/power-sweep-2026-10-24/serve-results.json) / [`tensor-results.jsonl`](../benchmarks/power-sweep-2026-10-24/tensor-results.jsonl) / [`samples.csv`](../benchmarks/power-sweep-2026-10-24/samples.csv), driver scripts alongside, chart [`power_sweep_chart.py`](../scripts/bench/power_sweep_chart.py)). Findings: (1) **the unlock's full 2× is real but power-bought** — fp16 / bf16 120 / 122 TFLOPS at 400 W vs stock 60 / 61, 75 / 81 at 260 W (the card holds ~1,350 MHz), 42 / 44 at 200 W (~900 MHz, below stock-at-260). (2) **Decode is bandwidth-bound and saturates:** 1K 70.3 → 72.7 → 74.7 tok/s at 260 / 300 / 400 W (+3.4 / +6.3 %), 128K 57.9 → 60.0 → 61.0; above ~320 W every 10 W buys < 0.5 %. (3) **Prefill is where the tensor rate goes:** 128K TTFT 6.92 → 6.07 → 5.48 s (−12 / −21 %), 32K 4.38 → 3.60 → 3.11 s. (4) **Heat is linear in the cap:** GPU 1 (upper card) 69 → 74 → 87 °C max, DDR5 DIMMs 49.8 → 52.0 → 56.5 °C against the 55 °C SPD alarm — the DIMMs cross it from ~370 W within a 3-min-per-level ascending sweep, which is what the earlier 350 W rejection saw. **Pick: 300 W** — the knee of both serving curves (about half the decode and 60 % of the TTFT gain available above 260 W, for +23 W mean draw per card), GPU 1 at 74 °C, DIMMs 3 °C under the alarm.
