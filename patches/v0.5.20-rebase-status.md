## Launch-blocking CLI retirement found by the parse check (fixed in `launch.sh`, stack-neutral)
Upstream `db272201a2` "[Config] Retire get_global_server_args, and clear the deprecated flags that
have a replacement (#38375)" dropped 23 CLI aliases. Two were on **every** preset's command line:
`--disable-piecewise-cuda-graph` (all 21, fleet-wide pin at the bottom of `launch.sh` plus per-preset
copies) and `--cuda-graph-max-bs 1` (13 presets) — on v0.5.20 the latter dies at argparse as
*"ambiguous option: --cuda-graph-max-bs could match --cuda-graph-max-bs-decode,
--cuda-graph-max-bs-prefill"*, so no preset would have booted. Both were already deprecated
aliases on v0.5.18 (`DeprecatedStoreConstAction` → `cuda_graph_backend_prefill=disabled` /
`dest=cuda_graph_max_bs_decode`), so `launch.sh`, the three `scripts/bench/b1*` arm scripts and
`rules-for-agents.md` now spell the canonical **`--cuda-graph-backend-prefill disabled`** /
**`--cuda-graph-max-bs-decode 1`**. Proof it is stack-neutral: for all 21 presets the old and new
argv parse to an **identical argparse namespace on v0.5.18** (so the running queue's later presets
launch unchanged), and the new argv parses on v0.5.20 (21/21, `prepare_server_args`, raw record).
`--disable-cuda-graph` (qwen36-vl-reap only) is still accepted on v0.5.20 (deprecated
`DeprecatedStoreTrueAction`, sets `disable_cuda_graph` — not a parse-time synonym of the backend
flags, left alone). Of the other 29 flags the presets use, none moved.
