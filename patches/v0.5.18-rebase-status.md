## Follow-ups surfaced by the audit (non-blocking, not v0.5.18 regressions)
- **041/058 + upstream's new stream-end drain.** v0.5.18 adds `BaseFormatDetector.finish()` /
  `FunctionCallParser.parse_stream_end()`, called on the terminal delta (`serving_chat.py`
  `flush=True`). `MistralDetector` does not override `finish()`, so a tool-name prefix still
  held in `_buffer` at EOS is dropped — the same outcome as v0.5.17 (no drain existed at all),
  but upstream now provides the sanctioned fix (a ~6-line `finish()` flushing `_buffer` as
  text, templates in `muse_glimmer_detector.py` / `kimik3_detector.py`). Add as an extra hunk
  to the 041/058 upstream-PR package.
- **029 covers the ConditionalGeneration `load_weights` only.** The text-only route
  (`qwen3_5_text.Qwen3_5MoeForCausalLM` → body `qwen3_5.Qwen3_5MoeForCausalLM.load_weights`)
  still drops `shared_expert_gate.weight_scale` via `ignore_suffixes` and warns on
  `weight_packed` — gate stays random-init. Pre-existing (identical in v0.5.17); dormant
  because no preset serves a CT MoE checkpoint declaring `Qwen3_5MoeForCausalLM`
  (`qwen36-dense` is the only CT preset and has no gate). Check `architectures` before
  pointing a preset at a CT MoE dir.
- **004 / 039 top-level remap / 051 / 029 are dormant on the current fleet** (no shipped
  checkpoint exercises them) — kept because they are cheap, but they cannot be validated by
  the fleet campaign; retirement candidates if their checkpoint shapes never return.
