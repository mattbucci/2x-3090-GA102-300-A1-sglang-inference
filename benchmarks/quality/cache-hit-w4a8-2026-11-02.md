# Why a prefix-cache hit is not a fresh prefill on `qwen38` — 2026-11-02

Issue #10 recorded that greedy outputs after a prefix-cache hit diverge from a fresh prefill on both hybrid
presets (first-token Δlogp up to 2–3 nats). Four arms of `scripts/eval/cache_hit_consistency.py` on `qwen38`
(8 cells: 4 × ~40K + 4 × ~165K actual prompt tokens; A = single-turn full hit, B = follow-up turn on the
cached conversation; "worse" = the hit diverges earlier than two fresh runs diverge from each other):

| arm | A worse | B worse | max first-token Δlogp A / B | A exact | B exact |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| W4A8 on, chunked prefill 8192 (the shipped preset) | 4 | 3 | 2.03 / 1.95 | 4/8 | 5/8 |
| W4A8 on, chunked prefill 2048 | 2 | 3 | 0.15 / 0.06 | 6/8 | 5/8 |
| W4A8 on + patch 076 (fp32 track snapshot) | 4 | 4 | 2.03 / 1.93 | 4/8 | 4/8 |
| **W4A8 off** (+ patch 076) | **1** | **2** | **0.13 / 0.11** | **7/8** | **6/8** |

Fresh-vs-fresh was exact in every cell of every arm (the floor is 0.0 nats), so none of this is run-to-run noise.

**Mechanism.** Patch 071 (W4A8 prefill) runs the dense GEMMs with per-token INT8 activations only for calls
with ≥ 2048 rows (`SGLANG_AWQ_W4A8_PREFILL_MIN_TOKENS`). A fresh fill of a 40K prompt in 8192-token chunks
runs its last chunk (7,341 rows) through INT8 activations; a cache hit restores the state at the last
64-token boundary and re-prefills a remainder of 9–191 tokens through the W4A16 Marlin path. The tail of the
prompt — the tokens that set the next token — is therefore computed with two different numerics, and the
greedy continuation moves from token 0 in half the cells. The 2048-chunk arm confirms it from the other side:
there the fresh fill's last chunk (40109 mod 2048 = 1,197 rows) is itself below the threshold and runs W4A16,
and hit-vs-fresh collapses to the 0.1-nat residual. With W4A8 off both paths are W4A16 and the residual is the
same 0.1 nat.

**What patch 076 is and is not.** The radix cache snapshots a GDN state at a 64-token boundary by copying the
chunk kernel's per-chunk `h`, which is allocated in the activation dtype (fp16 here); the fresh path carries
fp32. Upstream's in-kernel fp32 snapshot (`track_state`) was wired only for the KDA wrappers; patch 076
plumbs it through `chunk_gated_delta_rule` and the Triton GDN wrapper. The unit test
(`scripts/eval/test_gdn_track_snapshot.py`) shows the snapshot bit-exact against a boundary-truncated fresh
prefill, with the old `h` copy ~1e-3 off on a state of magnitude ~0.5; a one-cell served run with a
temporary path log confirmed the branch is taken. Its effect on greedy outputs is nil next to the W4A8 term
(table row 3), so it ships as a free correctness fix, not as the answer to #10.

**Residual (W4A8 off): 1 + 2 of 16 cells, ≤ 0.13 nats.** That is the chunk-alignment / fp16 reduction-order
class: the same instrument run under two chunk sizes with fp32 state carried in both moves fresh outputs by as
much (`scripts/eval/cache_hit_crossarm.py` over the 8192 and 2048 receipts: cross-run fresh diverges in 5/8 A
and 6/8 B cells, Δlogp up to 2.8 nats — the W4A8 tail term again, since the two chunkings put different row
counts through INT8).

**Consequences.**
- In an agentic session on `qwen38` every turn after the first runs its tail through W4A16 while the first
  fill ran INT8; the serving numerics are mixed by construction. The single-turn probes (needle 6/6, the 256K
  reasoning probe 100 % at 131K/256K, MMLU/HumanEval which never reach 2048 rows) cannot see this. The
  `qwen38` 196/300 SWE-bench cell predates W4A8 (shipped 2026-11-01); a W4A8-on cell against it is the real
  gate — tracked as a GitHub issue.
- Reading `cache_hit_consistency.py` on a W4A8 preset: compare hits against a fresh fill whose tail went
  through the same kernel, or run the instrument with W4A8 off to isolate the cache path.
- `nemotron3-omni` (moe_wna16, no W4A8 path) showed 8/12 divergent cells on 2026-11-02 with a non-zero
  fresh-vs-fresh floor; its Mamba2 path has a separate `recompute` snapshot route — not covered here.
