# Why a prefix-cache hit is not a fresh prefill on `qwen38` — 2026-11-02

Issue #10 recorded that greedy outputs after a prefix-cache hit diverge from a fresh prefill on both hybrid
presets (first-token Δlogp up to 2–3 nats). Four arms of `scripts/eval/cache_hit_consistency.py` on `qwen38`
(8 cells: 4 × ~40K + 4 × ~165K actual prompt tokens; A = single-turn full hit, B = follow-up turn on the
cached conversation; "worse" = the hit diverges earlier than two fresh runs diverge from each other):

| arm | A worse | B worse | max first-token Δlogp A / B | A exact | B exact |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| W4A8 on, chunked prefill 8192 (the shipped preset) | 4 | 3 | 2.03 / 1.95 | 4/8 | 5/8 |
| W4A8 on, chunked prefill 2048 | 2 | 3 | 0.15 / 0.06 | 6/8 | 5/8 |
| W4A8 on + patch 076 (fp32 track snapshot) | 4 | 4 | 2.03 / 1.93 | 4/8 | 4/8 |
| **W4A8 off** (+ patch 076) | **1** | **2** | **0.13 / 0.11** | **7/8** | **6/8** |

Fresh-vs-fresh was exact in every cell of every arm (the floor is 0.0 nats), so none of this is run-to-run noise.

**Mechanism.** Patch 071 (W4A8 prefill) runs the dense GEMMs with per-token INT8 activations only for calls
with ≥ 2048 rows (`SGLANG_AWQ_W4A8_PREFILL_MIN_TOKENS`). A fresh fill of a 40K prompt in 8192-token chunks
runs its last chunk (7,341 rows) through INT8 activations; a cache hit restores the state at the last
64-token boundary and re-prefills a remainder of 9–191 tokens through the W4A16 Marlin path. The tail of the
prompt — the tokens that set the next token — is therefore computed with two different numerics, and the
greedy continuation moves from token 0 in half the cells. The 2048-chunk arm confirms it from the other side:
there the fresh fill's last chunk (40109 mod 2048 = 1,197 rows) is itself below the threshold and runs W4A16,
and hit-vs-fresh collapses to the 0.1-nat residual. With W4A8 off both paths are W4A16 and the residual is the
same 0.1 nat.

**What patch 076 is and is not.** The radix cache snapshots a GDN state at a 64-token boundary by copying the
chunk kernel's per-chunk `h`, which is allocated in the activation dtype (fp16 here); the fresh path carries
fp32. Upstream's in-kernel fp32 snapshot (`track_state`) was wired only for the KDA wrappers; patch 076
plumbs it through `chunk_gated_delta_rule` and the Triton GDN wrapper. The unit test
(`scripts/eval/test_gdn_track_snapshot.py`) shows the snapshot bit-exact against a boundary-truncated fresh
prefill, with the old `h` copy ~1e-3 off on a state of magnitude ~0.5; a one-cell served run with a
temporary path log confirmed the branch is taken. Its effect on greedy outputs is nil next to the W4A8 term
(table row 3), so it ships as a free correctness fix, not as the answer to #10.

**Residual (W4A8 off): 1 + 2 of 16 cells, ≤ 0.13 nats.** That is the chunk-alignment / fp16 reduction-order
class: the same instrument run under two chunk sizes with fp32 state carried in both moves fresh outputs by as
much (`scripts/eval/cache_hit_crossarm.py` over the 8192 and 2048 receipts: cross-run fresh diverges in 5/8 A
and 6/8 B cells, Δlogp up to 2.8 nats — the W4A8 tail term again, since the two chunkings put different row
counts through INT8).

**Consequences.**
- In an agentic session on `qwen38` every turn after the first runs its tail through W4A16 while the first
  fill ran INT8; the serving numerics are mixed by construction. The single-turn probes (needle 6/6, the 256K
  reasoning probe 100 % at 131K/256K, MMLU/HumanEval which never reach 2048 rows) cannot see this. The
  `qwen38` 196/300 SWE-bench cell predates W4A8 (shipped 2026-11-01); a W4A8-on cell against it is the real
  gate — tracked as a GitHub issue.
- Reading `cache_hit_consistency.py` on a W4A8 preset: compare hits against a fresh fill whose tail went
  through the same kernel, or run the instrument with W4A8 off to isolate the cache path.
- `nemotron3-omni` (moe_wna16, no W4A8 path) showed 8/12 divergent cells on 2026-11-02 with a non-zero
  fresh-vs-fresh floor; its Mamba2 path has a separate `recompute` snapshot route — not covered here.

Receipts: `cache-hit-consistency-qwen38-{chunk8192,chunk2048,p076,w4a8off}-2026-11-02.json` (texts and
first-token top-5 per cell), unit-test log in the chain46 record, patch `patches/076-gdn-fp32-track-state-snapshot.patch`.

## Mitigation tried and rejected: uniform INT8 for every extend ≥ 64 rows (2026-11-03)

`SGLANG_AWQ_W4A8_PREFILL_MIN_TOKENS=64` on `qwen38`, same session as the shipped 2048 threshold
(`bench_cache_hit_extend.py`, medians of 4; `cache-hit-extend-qwen38-w4a8min{64,2048}-2026-11-03.json`):

| turn (new tokens) at cached prefix | 35K: 2048 → 64 | 142K: 2048 → 64 | 217K: 2048 → 64 |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| 1 | 61 → 98 ms | 69 → 111 ms | 113 → 150 ms |
| 64 | 127 → 159 ms | 278 → 311 ms | 436 → 479 ms |
| 128 | 127 → 154 ms | 331 → 356 ms | 483 → 512 ms |

+30–40 ms per turn (the per-call INT8 weight conversion on short GEMMs; the 1-token turn pays too because the
captured 8–128-token prefill graph no longer covers the INT8 path), and the hits get **less** exact, not more:
`cache-hit-consistency-qwen38-w4a8min64-2026-11-03.json` has 5/8 single-turn and 6/8 follow-up cells worse
than the fresh floor, follow-up turns diverging at 3–13 tokens. The INT8 GEMM's result therefore depends on the
row count itself (tile / split-K accumulation order), so no threshold makes a hit reproduce a fresh fill. The
shipped 2048 threshold stays; the agentic-quality question is issue #20's bake-off cell. The shipped-threshold
turn numbers match the 2026-11-01 receipt of the same probe (no regression from patches 075/076).

## Chunk-size invariance of the W4A16 path (2026-11-03): the perturbation is the INT8 path's

`qwen38`, W4A8 off, chunked prefill 2048 vs 8192 (`cache_hit_crossarm.py` over
`cache-hit-consistency-qwen38-w4a8off-2026-11-02.json` and `…-w4a8off-chunk2048-2026-11-03.json`):

| | hits worse than floor (A / B of 8) | cross-run fresh worse than floor (A / B) | max cross-run first-token Δlogp |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| W4A8 on (8192 vs 2048) | 4 / 3 | 5 / 6 | 2.82 nats |
| **W4A8 off (8192 vs 2048)** | **1 / 2** | **1 / 2** | **0.14 nats** |

The 2048-chunk W4A8-off run is itself 16/16 exact on hits. With fp16 activations the prefill is, to within
0.14 nats on the first token, invariant to how the rows are batched; with INT8 activations the same prompt
moves by up to 2.8 nats depending on which rows share a chunk. That is a property of the W4A8 lever, not of
the cache: the per-token INT8 quantisation of the prompt tail produces greedy-flip-level perturbations that
depend on batching, and the single-turn gates that passed W4A8 (needle, the 256K reasoning probe, MMLU/HE)
were insensitive to it. The agentic-quality verdict is still issue #20's full-300 cell, but the prior now
leans toward W4A8 being a measurable perturbation on `qwen38`; the W4A8 defaults on `qwen36-dense`,
`gemma4-31b`, `qwen35-moe` and `qwen36` carry the same question.

## `nemotron3-omni` (Mamba2 hybrid, moe_wna16, no W4A8) — hits are no worse than a re-chunk (2026-11-03)

Same instrument, default chunk vs 2048 (`cache-hit-consistency-nemotron3-omni-2026-11-03.json`,
`…-chunk2048-2026-11-03.json`, `cache_hit_crossarm.py`): fresh-vs-fresh is exact in 14/16 cells (two
follow-up cells show a non-zero floor, 0.02 and 1.4 nats — a residual nondeterminism this preset has and
`qwen38` does not), hits diverge before the floor in 4/8 single-turn and 5/8 follow-up cells, and **a fresh
fill under the other chunk size diverges in 4/8 and 6/8 cells with first-token Δlogp up to 6.7 nats**. The
Mamba2 boundary snapshot is fp32 end to end (`chunk_state_varlen` writes the pool dtype), so the hit term is
the same row-count dependence of the serving kernels that any re-chunking shows — on this preset the
`moe_wna16` fused-MoE path and the SSD scan, not W4A8. A cache hit on `nemotron3-omni` is therefore exactly
as reproducible as its own prefill is, and no more; nothing cache-side to fix. The large Δlogp values are a
property of this checkpoint/kernel pair worth remembering when reading its agentic cells.
