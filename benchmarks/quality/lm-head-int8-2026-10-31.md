# INT8 `lm_head` through GPTQ-Marlin W8A16 — patch 069 (2026-10-31)

**Why:** on the M=1 agentic profile the fp16 `lm_head` gemv is ~10 % of the decode step at DRAM roofline
([profile](../qwen38-agentic-workload-profile-2026-09-10.md)); our AWQ ships leave the head in bf16
(1.27 GB/rank on qwen38: 248,320 × 5,120). Halving its bytes was the ranked lever after the Flint KV dtype.

**What:** `SGLANG_LM_HEAD_INT8=1` (default ON in `launch.sh` since 2026-10-31) — `ParallelLMHead` wraps its unquantized
method; after loading, the bf16/fp16 head is quantized per output channel (symmetric int8, `uint8b128` storage),
GPTQ-packed on the device and repacked with `gptq_marlin_repack` (the kernel the FP8-on-Ampere path uses) into Marlin
W8A16; `LogitsProcessor` dispatches through `quant_method.apply` → `apply_gptq_marlin_linear` (channelwise, no zero
points). `tie_weights()` reverts to the dense method, so tied heads (Gemma 4) are untouched. Patch
[`069-lm-head-int8-marlin.patch`](../../patches/069-lm-head-int8-marlin.patch), 3-gate clean.
