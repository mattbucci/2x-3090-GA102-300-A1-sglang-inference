# INT8 `lm_head` through GPTQ-Marlin W8A16 — patch 069 (2026-10-31)

**Why:** on the M=1 agentic profile the fp16 `lm_head` gemv is ~10 % of the decode step at DRAM roofline
([profile](../qwen38-agentic-workload-profile-2026-09-10.md)); our AWQ ships leave the head in bf16
(1.27 GB/rank on qwen38: 248,320 × 5,120). Halving its bytes was the ranked lever after the Flint KV dtype.

**What:** `SGLANG_LM_HEAD_INT8=1` (default ON in `launch.sh` since 2026-10-31) — `ParallelLMHead` wraps its unquantized
method; after loading, the bf16/fp16 head is quantized per output channel (symmetric int8, `uint8b128` storage),
GPTQ-packed on the device and repacked with `gptq_marlin_repack` (the kernel the FP8-on-Ampere path uses) into Marlin
W8A16; `LogitsProcessor` dispatches through `quant_method.apply` → `apply_gptq_marlin_linear` (channelwise, no zero
points). `tie_weights()` reverts to the dense method, so tied heads (Gemma 4) are untouched. Patch
[`069-lm-head-int8-marlin.patch`](../../patches/069-lm-head-int8-marlin.patch), 3-gate clean.

## Fleet quality — the first reading was an instrument mismatch (corrected 2026-10-31 07:40)

The "regressions" first recorded here (qwen36 MMLU 5/57 vs 53/57, qwen36-dense 1/57, qwen35-moe 1/57, coder-30b 38/57,
gemma4 HumanEval 0/40) compared today's `eval_quality.py` numbers against receipts made by a **different harness**:
the `*-v0522.json` / `*-unlock.json` flip receipts come from `eval_and_chart.py` (MMLU and HumanEval at
`max_tokens 4096`, answer letter read from reasoning + content, HumanEval through chat), while `eval_quality.py` caps
MMLU at 200 tokens, reads the letter from content only, and runs HumanEval through raw completions. For a thinking
model whose think is 130–270 tokens on an easy MC question (measured on the 10-20 image: 135–185 tokens, correct
answers), MMLU-200 is a knife-edge that says nothing about the head. Three controls proved it: the **bf16 head on
today's tree scores the same** (qwen36 4/57, 31/40); **patch 067 reverted** scores the same (5/57, 31/40); and the
**2026-10-20 flip image itself** — the exact stack of the 28/30 receipt — scores 5/57, 30/40 today under
`eval_quality.py`. The serving stack is unchanged; the comparison was invalid. (gemma4's 0/40 on raw completions
is the same instrument failing a thinking-on template; its sampled needle today is 12/12.)

What the same-session, same-instrument pairs actually show so far: qwen38 bf16 37/40 vs int8 36/40, MMLU 39/57
both; qwen36 bf16 31/40 vs int8 30/40, MMLU 4–5/57 both (the instrument's floor). The argmax-sensitivity argument
above remains a reason to gate carefully, not evidence of harm. **The proper gate — `eval_and_chart.py` MMLU +
HumanEval, bf16 vs int8 in the same session — runs next for qwen36, coder-30b-eval, qwen36-dense, qwen35-moe and
qwen38, and the default follows its result.**
